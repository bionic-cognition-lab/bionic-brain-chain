Bionic Brain Chain

Five LLM agents, each modeled on a brain region, pass a question down a fixed pipeline. Each one may cite only its own small micro-textbook, and the last station fact-checks everything before it answers.

The stations

The question enters at the Commander and passes through every station in order. Each station's built-in bias comes from the platform's fixed role contract.
